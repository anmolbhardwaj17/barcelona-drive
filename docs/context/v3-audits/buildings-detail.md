# v3 audit — Roofs, shopfronts, awnings, terraces, rooftop clutter
**VERDICT: REFACTOR**
## Justification
REFACTOR, with one honest DELETE and one geometry-level rebuild inside it. I am not manufacturing a rewrite because the two things that are hard here are already correct.

What is already right and would cost days to re-derive: (1) the roof material architecture. `getRoofMaterialKey()` returning the constant `'roof_FFFFFF'` with the palette baked into vertex colours (buildingWorker.js:392-394, :1138) means the ENTIRE city roofscape is one material and one texture. Every other domain is fighting variant explosion; this one already solved it. A rebuild would land on the same design. (2) the four-renderer co-registration. `shopSegSkipped` + `shopGroundY` (shopfrontRenderer.js:31-38, imported by awningRenderer.js:15) make shopfront, awning, sign and terrace agree on the same edge, the same segment indices and the same quantized ground height. Changelog.md:1018 records that this was converged with the user across a full session of jitter bugs. Throwing it away to "start clean" makes the end result worse, not better.

What is genuinely missing is texture coordinates and props — additive work on a sound skeleton, not a dead end. The roof path in particular is a rounding error away from working: `ensureUvs` (buildingWorker.js:657-661) writes a zero array, and replacing that with a planar XZ projection is one function.

THE DELETE (no-slacking applied): buildingWorker.js `awningGeoms` (:1364-1374), `signboardGeoms` (:1377-1388) and `pillarGeoms` (:1341-1361) must go. They are Delhi-era, they are the only remaining un-gated members of a block whose siblings were disabled at :1392 and :1156, and they geometrically intersect the Barcelona renderers on exactly the arterial buildings the player drives past. Patching them to coexist is not possible — two systems dressing the same 3 m of facade at overlapping heights is a design error, not a tuning error.

THE INTERNAL REBUILD: cafeTerraceRenderer's furniture geometry (:47-79) — 144 tris of untextured cylinders and boxes — cannot reach the ETS2 bar by any amount of patching, because the bar is "textured prop from a library" and this is "primitives with baked vertex colour". Replace the geometry; keep `tooCloseToRoad` and the placement loop verbatim.

ANSWER TO THE POSED QUESTION — yes, roof geometry is decisively EASIER than facade texturing, and it should be sequenced first. Roofs bypass the entire facade prerequisite chain: no per-category variant explosion (one bucket vs 8 categories x hero x palette, meshMaterializer.js:966-984); no AO attribute and therefore no v9 grid dependency and no pre-v9 null path (buildingWorker.js:1069-1091 builds ao/wash for walls only); no UV unwrap (a horizontal cap takes uv=(x/T, z/T) in one line); no `makeBoxGeom` 8→24 rework (v2 item 2.5); no winding/FrontSide fix (2.6); no FLOOR_HEIGHT period fix (2.4); no night window-emissive atlas. And NO RE-BAKE — everything here derives from footprint + height, already present in v7/v8/v9. The full roof programme (UVs, texture, universal parapet, clutter) is 5.0 days with a hard dependency only on the shared art/KTX2 pipeline. Facade texturing is 4+ days of prerequisites before the first pixel changes.
## ETS2 reference
ETS2 (SCS prism3d, .pmd/.pmg prefab kits) at the "not too good but better than ours" calibration:

ROOFS. Every city building prefab carries a separate flat roof cap mesh plus a REAL modelled parapet, ~0.9-1.5 m tall, 3-4 quads in section (outer face / coping / inner face). Not a normal-map fake — it is the silhouette against the sky and it must survive at grazing angles. The coping band gets its own UV row on the shared building sheet. Roof surface is a low-density tiling material: 1024 diffuse + normal, roughly 2-4 texel/cm at roof scale, which VISIBLY repeats every 3-4 m and nobody minds at driving speed. Their asphalt/gravel roof is not a hero asset.

ROOFTOP CLUTTER. 3-8 props per roof drawn from a shared ~30-piece city prop library: AC condenser bank, vent/chimney stack, satellite dish, water tank, stair penthouse, roof hatch, railing runs. Each prop is 60-200 tris with a 128-256 px region of one shared 1K atlas (albedo + normal, spec/gloss not metal/rough). Critically: clutter is its own LOD group that CULLS ENTIRELY at ~100-150 m, leaving the parapet silhouette plus a flat texture beyond that. That cull is the whole trick and it is exactly what a browser can afford.

SHOPFRONTS. Ground floors are a separate modular strip mesh with its own material — display glass with a BAKED INTERIOR CARD plane behind it (textured backdrop geometry, not a shader trick), a stallriser/kickplate band, and an alternate mesh variant with a CLOSED ROLLER SHUTTER. Fascia sign is a flat plane pulled from a sign atlas. Corner buildings get shopfronts on BOTH street faces.

AWNINGS. 6-10 triangles: sloped top plane, closed end gussets, vertical valance. Fabric texture (stripes/solid) with alpha only on a scalloped valance edge. No cloth sim, no per-frame anything.

TERRACES. Prop clusters from the same shared library — table+chairs group, parasol (open and closed variants), planter, glass wind barrier. Textured, ~150-250 tris per cluster.

Calibration point: an ETS2 AC unit is a ~120-tri box on a 128 px atlas cell. That is the bar. It is low. We are below it because we have literally zero of it.
## Current state
ROOFS — architecturally ideal, artistically empty.
- One earcut cap per building: `createPolygonRoofBuffers` buildingWorker.js:592-609 (cylinder disc at :615-624), `ROOF_Y_OFFSET = 0.03` (:34).
- ALL roofs in the city go into ONE material bucket: `getRoofMaterialKey()` returns the literal constant `'roof_FFFFFF'` (buildingWorker.js:392-394). Palette is baked into the vertex-colour attribute (`getRoofTint` :372-378, applied :1138-1140), so `getRoofMaterial` (meshMaterializer.js:682-690) is a single `MeshLambertMaterial({color:0xFFFFFF, vertexColors:true, side:BUILDING_SIDE})`. → 1 roof draw per tile, ~12 city-wide.
- ROOF UVs ARE ALL ZERO. `ensureUvs` (buildingWorker.js:657-661) allocates `new Float32Array(vertCount*2)` and returns. Any texture on the roof material today samples one texel. This is the single blocking defect.

## Risks
- Rooftop clutter is INVISIBLE at street level once parapets exist — that is the point (it is the ETS2 trick), but it means the payoff only lands in drone mode and from Montjuic / Ronda de Dalt. If it is sized or reviewed as if it is always on screen it will be judged not worth 2.0 days. Gate it hard off the existing `altMult` (tileManager.js:2899) and review it from the drone camera, not the driving camera.
- Extending shopfronts/awnings/signs from longest-edge-only to all street-facing edges multiplies segments by ~2-3x on chamfered Eixample corner blocks. SEG_CAP 260 (shopfrontRenderer.js:23) is first-come across the tile's building list, so it will start clipping mid-block and produce visibly half-dressed facades — worse than the current uniform under-dressing. The cap must become per-building-fair in the same commit, not as a follow-up.
- Deleting the duplicate worker awning/signboard/pillar blocks changes the look of every `commercial` building (untagged buildings on primary/secondary roads — the arterial frontages). That is a look change requiring screenshot approval, not a silent cleanup commit.
- The four renderers are co-registered ONLY by convention: shopfrontRenderer exports `shopSegSkipped` and `shopGroundY` and awningRenderer imports them (awningRenderer.js:15), but shopSignRenderer and cafeTerraceRenderer each re-derive the longest edge independently and shopSignRenderer does NOT quantize ground Y (:150). Changing the edge-selection rule in one place and not the others reintroduces the row-jitter bug that changelog.md:1018 records was already fixed once.
- Height constants across the four renderers are hard-coded and must move together with any facade change: KICK_TOP 0.42 / GLASS_TOP 2.55 / LINTEL_TOP 2.80 (shopfrontRenderer.js:17-19), AWN_TOP_Y 2.9 (awningRenderer.js:14), SIGN_Y 3.15 (shopSignRenderer.js:15) — all sized against WINDOW_STYLES `marginB` 3.8-4.0 (meshMaterializer.js:37-46). If the facade domain retunes the ground-floor band these silently misalign with no error.
- Once any of these meshes carries a city-wide shared atlas, the tile-unload disposal at tileManager.js:2856-2872 becomes fatal: it DISPOSES material and material.map unless `userData.sharedMaterial` is set, and neither the shopfront FRAME mesh (shopfrontRenderer.js:155) nor the awning mesh (awningRenderer.js:151) sets it. Correct today (per-tile materials), destructive the moment a shared texture lands. The v2 fix (invert to an explicit `ownedMaterial` opt-in) is a hard prerequisite, not a nice-to-have.
- `GLOBAL_VERTEX_BUDGET = 100000` (buildingWorker.js:873) drops entire buildings at :1098 and drops the roof cap alone at :1148 with no degrade path. Parapets and coping add to the same merged wall/roof accounting, raising the chance of hitting it in the p90=306 / max=745-building tiles. A hollow open-topped building under `BUILDING_SIDE = DoubleSide` is a far worse artifact than a missing parapet.
- The roof texture will multiply against `_ROOF_NIGHT_TINT` (meshMaterializer.js:680) AND the baked vertex-colour palette. The night tint was converged with the user over three separate sessions (changelog.md:1034,1036,1038) to stop the peach palette going pink-maroon under the blue night rig. Adding a sampled texture into that product needs a night screenshot check or it re-opens a settled argument.
