# v3 audit — Player car, traffic, parked cars, pedestrians (car/*.js, ui/carShowcase.js, public/models/{cars,people}, bmw_m3.glb)
**VERDICT: REBUILD**
## Justification
Not a stylistic call — a measurable structural one. Every asset in this domain has no usable texture space:

- bmw_m3.glb: TEXCOORD_0 is (0,0) on all 19,712 vertices across all 17 car nodes.
- Kenney fleet: 59 unique UVs per body, snapped to a 1/32 palette grid.
- People GLBs: three of five have no TEXCOORD_0 attribute at all, and carModels.js:221 discards UVs on the other two anyway.
- Zero image data across all 15 GLBs except one 3,110-byte 512x512 palette.

The ETS2 bar for this subsystem is "normal-mapped body panels, a paintjob mask driving colour variety, correct proportions." Every one of those requires a UV unwrap. You cannot patch a UV unwrap onto shipped vertex data. There is no amount of shader work, material tier, lighting, or re-skinning that reaches the bar from here — v2-plan-hardened item 2.10's "re-skin the 9 Kenney cars, 1.5 d, low risk" is not achievable as written and should be struck.

Two independent facts make the rebuild pay for itself rather than cost:
1. The domain currently burns 1.08 M triangles (41% of the city budget) and ~104-194 draws (36-67% of the measured frame) on assets with no texels. A modular 6-body kit at 1,800-2,800 tris LOD0 / 500-700 LOD1, instanced with a paintjob mask, lands at ~420 k tris and ~40 draws while looking dramatically better. The rebuild is a NET REDUCTION on every budget axis.
2. Download is already 7.09 MB, of which 4.59 MB is people GLBs whose animation and skinning data is discarded at load (baked to 10 static frames). Deleting the fantasy adventurer and the punk alone frees 3.09 MB — which pays for the entire vehicle texture set with change left over.

Scope of the rebuild, precisely: REBUILD the traffic/parked fleet as a shared-atlas modular kit; REBUILD the player car's asset (re-UV + texture the existing 9,792-tri geometry — the mesh and proportions are fine, only the UVs and maps are missing, so this is asset work, not a code rewrite); REBUILD pedestrians smaller (4 realistic city bodies, one atlas, UVs carried through the flipbook) and DELETE adventurer.glb and punk.glb outright. KEEP all the runtime systems — the path-following/chaining traffic AI (trafficSystem.js:157-191), the deterministic parked-car placement with cached segment metadata (parkedCars.js:118-197), the flipbook instancing architecture (pedestrians.js:155-262), the contact-shadow pool, the knockdown mechanic. Those are good, perf-tuned code and they are asset-agnostic. This is an asset rebuild with a thin rewiring layer, not a system rewrite. Recommending a code rewrite here would be the rewrite-for-its-own-sake failure mode.

The blocking precondition is a decision I do not own: rallyStyle.js:8 currently ships art-of-rally as the default. That style and the ETS2 target are mutually exclusive. Every work item below assumes ETS2 wins.
## ETS2 reference
ETS2/Prism3D, calibrated to "not too good but better than ours":

TRAFFIC VEHICLES. Each AI car is a hand-modelled body at roughly 8-15k tris LOD0, dropping through 3-4 LODs (~4k / ~1.5k / ~500) with switch distances in the 25 m / 60 m / 130 m range and a hard cull near 400-500 m. Texture set per vehicle family: one ~1024x1024 or 2048x1024 diffuse, one normal map (usually 1K), and a packed spec/gloss map. The critical trick is the PAINTJOB MASK: the body diffuse is authored greyscale/white in the paint region with a mask channel, so a single atlas drives 10+ body colours per model at zero extra VRAM. Wheels/tyres/glass/lights live in a shared region so the whole fleet is a small number of materials. Result: modest polycount, real normal-mapped panel/shutline detail, correct European proportions, wide colour variety, few draw calls.

PARKED / STATIC VEHICLES. Same asset, LOD1 or LOD2 only, instanced along authored parking prefabs. Grounded by real shadow-map shadows (their casters are cheap because the cascade is tight).

PLAYER VEHICLE. Highest texel budget in the game: 2K albedo + 2K normal + spec, modelled shutlines, glass with tint + interior shell visible through it, plates, tyre sidewall lettering, and an env-cube reflection on the paint (their `truckpaint` shader). Their player vehicle is the one asset that justifies its cost because it is on screen 100% of the time.

NIGHT. This is what actually defines the ETS2 look and it is NOT achieved with punctual lights per car. Their traffic uses emissive light quads PLUS an additive beam card and an additive ground light-pool decal projected ahead of the vehicle. Tail lights are emissive quads with a small bloom. A string of red taillights and paired headlight pools is the entire night read.

PEDESTRIANS. ETS2/ATS has essentially NO walking crowd in the drivable world. Where pedestrians appear (newer city reworks, Iberia/HoR scenery prefabs) they are sparse, distant, non-interactive animated scenery props behind barriers, never something you can hit. Their answer to "city life" is parked cars, signage, street furniture, and traffic density — not people. This is directly relevant to our 168-strong knock-down-able crowd.

SCALE CORRECTNESS. Their proportions are measured from real vehicles. They never non-uniformly squash a model to make it "read as a car" — which is exactly what carModels.js:75 does.

## Risks
- SCHEDULE / SOURCING — the biggest risk. There is no CC0 library of textured, correctly-proportioned European city cars. Kenney is palette-atlas stylized (V-2), Quaternius is stylized, Poly Haven has no vehicles, Poly Pizza is untextured low-poly. The 6-body kit + atlas is ~11 days of one-person Blender authoring with no fallback. If throughput slips, the mitigation is to ship 3 bodies instead of 6 and lean on the paintjob mask for variety — 3 bodies x 10 colours still beats today's 9 bodies x 1 colour.
- ART DIRECTION IS ALREADY COMMITTED THE OTHER WAY. rallyStyle.js:8 ships art-of-rally as the DEFAULT, not as an option (V-5). The current flat untextured fleet is coherent inside that style; it is only 'wrong' against the ETS2 target. If this decision is not made explicitly and written into decisions.md before asset #1, we will author an ETS2 vehicle kit into an art-of-rally world and it will look worse than either does today. This blocks every P2 item and it is not my call.
- PAINTJOB MASK CAN RE-CREATE THE EXACT BUG THAT KILLED THE LAST TINT. trafficSystem.js:65-70 and parkedCars.js:104-106 both record a per-car multiply-tint being ripped out because it turned already-coloured bodies muddy. The mask approach only works if the atlas paint region is authored greyscale/white and the mask is exact. A sloppy mask reproduces the muddy-tint regression on the whole fleet at once.
- NON-UNIFORM SQUASH DOUBLE-APPLY. carModels.js:75 and :146 bake a (0.95, 0.82, 1.0) squash into every template (V-10). New models authored at true dimensions will be squashed a second time unless both lines are deleted in the same commit as the kit lands. Silent, and it will read as 'the new cars look wrong' rather than as a bug.
- PEDESTRIANS MAY NOT SURVIVE THE CALIBRATION AT ALL. ETS2's answer to city life is essentially no interactive crowd (V-15, medium confidence). 168 low-poly people at 150 m with no shadow-map shadows may still read as toys after the rebuild, having cost 5 days. KILL CRITERION: if the corridor seam test says peds hurt the ETS2 read, cut PED_CAP from 168 to ~60, confine them to the near sidewalk, and bank the triangles. Do not spend a second art pass on them.
- MESHSTANDARD CARVE-OUT MAY BE REFUSED. v2-plan-hardened dropped MeshStandard entirely on a measured budget argument. Vehicle bodies genuinely need specular response (V-16) and the player car already opts out via MeshPhysical (carModel.js:111). If the carve-out is refused, the fallback is MeshPhong (specular + normalMap, no IBL cost) — worse but survivable. If we are forced all the way down to Lambert the entire vehicle rebuild loses most of its value and the verdict should be revisited.
- MESHOPT REGRESSION RECURRENCE. loaders.js:4-5 records geometry compression breaking the car meshes. The recorded cause is quantization compounding through the load-time merge + matrix-transform in loadCarTemplate, not the codec (V-17). If the new kit is shipped pre-merged and pre-oriented this is safe; if anyone re-introduces load-time merging to 'save a draw call', the regression comes straight back.
- DRAW-CALL ACCOUNTING TRAP. renderer.info.render.calls counts a WEBGL_multi_draw batch as ONE (established fact), so instancing wins here will look larger in the counter than in the GPU timeline. Validate the traffic/parked rewire against p95 GPU ms at night, never against the draw counter alone.
- MY ~120-PARKED-CAR FIGURE IS DERIVED, NOT MEASURED. It comes from the Eixample 133 m block pitch against RANGE 200 m / SPACING 17.5 m / 0.55 fill (V-18). Everything else in this report is read out of vertex data or source lines; this one number is a model. A single live STATS capture in dense Eixample at night would confirm or move it, and it is the input to the parked-car triangle line of my budget ask.
- SECOND GL CONTEXT. carShowcase.js:12 stands up its own WebGLRenderer and its own copy of the hero car (V-14). Free today; costs a duplicated ~7 MiB texture set the moment the hero car is textured, because textures cannot be shared across contexts. Must be fixed in the same release as the hero car, not after.
