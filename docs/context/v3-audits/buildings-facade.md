# v3 audit — Building geometry + facades (workers/buildingWorker.js, workers/meshMaterializer.js, map/buildingRenderer.js)
**VERDICT: REBUILD**
## Justification
REBUILD the facade generation and the detail-budget model. KEEP the worker transport, the per-material-key merge and the vertex-colour tint collapse — that layer is good and a rewrite would reproduce it.

Why REFACTOR cannot reach the bar. The current look is produced by ~250 lines of canvas drawing (meshMaterializer.js:118-365) painting a 256x256 tile onto a 4-vertex wall quad. Every one of its load-bearing properties is wrong in a way that patching cannot fix: 21-25 texels/m against an 85-150 target (a canvas generator cannot get there — a 1024^2 canvas per category at boot already costs us 67 MB of backing store for the night atlases alone); no normal map and no plausible way to author one procedurally on the main thread; a 10 m UV period that structurally cannot align to a 3.5 m storey; and a wall that has no vertices between ground and roof, which is why the AO fade is broken, why the ground floor cannot be differentiated in geometry, and why window recesses are impossible. Fixing the FLOOR_HEIGHT wrap alone (v2 item 2.4, 1 day) removes the mid-air shopfront on 88.5% of buildings but leaves us with an untextured-looking 21 px/m wall — a real improvement that lands nowhere near ETS2.

Why REBUILD is not a rewrite for its own sake. The end result is different in kind: modular storey bands with authored albedo+normal+window-mask in a CompressedArrayTexture gives, in one change, correct storey alignment, 128 texels/m, real normal-mapped reveals, ground-floor vs upper-floor differentiation, per-building material variety without extra draws, correct AO vertices, and a night mask on the same UV layout. It also RECLAIMS 58.6 MiB of VRAM (the 16 canvas night atlases plus the 8 day canvases) — this is the only domain that hands budget back.

Why the detail path is also a rebuild, not a tuning pass. The measured outcome is that the median dense tile gives cornices and balconies to 26.6% of buildings and the worst give 8.5%. Raising BALCONY_VERT_CAP does not fix it: the cap exists because the railing costs 58 triangles per balcony. The fix is to replace the modelled railing with an alpha-cutout card (2 tris, 29x cheaper) and to change the caps from a first-come vertex race to a per-building proportional triangle budget. Then ~100% of buildings get the Eixample cornice and floor bands, which is the single largest cohesion win available in this domain.

What is DELETE. renderLODBuildings (buildingRenderer.js:2541-2650) — measured effectively unreachable after a tile finishes building; it costs CPU, a GPU upload and heap for a few frames of visibility. Also the 16 boot-time canvas night atlases, superseded by the authored mask array.
## ETS2 reference
ETS2 (Prism3D) city buildings, at the "not too good but better than ours" calibration:

GEOMETRY. Hand-modelled modular prefabs, ~1-4k tris each. A facade is NOT one quad floor-to-roof: it is a stack of vertical MODULES — a distinct ground-floor module (shopfront/door/plinth), N repeated upper-storey modules, and a crown module (cornice + parapet). Window openings are actual geometry, recessed ~15-25 cm, so reveals self-shadow at grazing angles down a street. Balcony/railing detail is alpha-cutout cards (2 tris + a mask), never modelled balusters. Roof is a separate textured plane with a parapet lip.

TEXTURES. Diffuse + tangent-space normal + a scalar specular; base ETS2 is NOT PBR — which maps almost exactly onto MeshLambert + normalMap, our existing capability. Facade sheets are typically 1024x1024 DXT1 covering ~8-12 m of wall, or 512x512 per 3.5 m storey module. That is 85-150 texels/metre. Weathering (vertical dirt runs under sills and cornices, damp at the plinth, sun-bleach at the top) is BAKED INTO THE ALBEDO — this is a large fraction of what reads as "the ETS2 look" and costs zero runtime. A separate single-channel light-mask drives lit windows at night, on the same UV layout as the albedo.

VARIETY. Per-block material variety comes from a handful of facade sheets (5-10) permuted across buildings, plus per-building tint, NOT from per-building unique textures. Palette is desaturated European: render, grey plaster, brick, concrete.

LOD. 3-4 discrete LOD meshes per prefab plus a distant block impostor; city draw distance ~500-800 m with aggressive per-object switching (not per-tile).

Deliberately NOT in the target: photoscan albedo, parallax-occlusion windows, per-window interior cubemaps, GI. Those are above the bar the user set.
## Current state
ARCHITECTURE (good, keep). Buildings are worker-generated: `workers/buildingWorker.js` emits pure typed-array buffers merged per material key; `workers/meshMaterializer.js:840-940` wraps them into meshes. `map/buildingRenderer.js` is dead for geometry (changelog.md:39) except `getNightEmissiveTexture` (:564) and `renderLODBuildings` (:2541). Palette colours are baked into vertex colours (`buildingWorker.js:338-355`) so one WHITE material serves a whole category — this collapsed ~200 draws to ~35 and is genuinely good design.

GEOMETRY. A wall face is ONE quad: 4 vertices, corners only, base and roofline (`workerGeometry.js:36-125`, `extrudePolygonWalls`). There is no storey structure at all. Roofs are one earcut cap (`buildingWorker.js:592-609`) into a SINGLE material key `roof_FFFFFF` (`:392`) whose material has NO map of any kind (`meshMaterializer.js:682-690`) — the largest horizontal surface in the game is flat vertex colour. `makeBoxGeom` (`workerGeometry.js:864-916`) is 8 verts with SMOOTH averaged normals and valid UVs on only 2 of 6 faces.

TEXTURES. Facade albedo is a 256x256 canvas drawn at runtime, one per category, 8 total (`meshMaterializer.js:118-365`). Over WALL_REPEAT_HORIZONTAL_M=12 / FLOOR_HEIGHT=10 that is 21.3 x 25.6 texels per metre — 4x to 7x below the ETS2 bar of 85-150. `tex.anisotropy` is never set (default 1) while road/terrain use 4 (`roadRenderer.js:209`, `terrainRenderer.js:31`), so facades mush at exactly the grazing angles an Eixample street presents. Zero normal maps, zero aoMap. No KTX2Loader or Basis transcoder exists anywhere in `frontend/src` or `frontend/public`.

THE FLOOR_HEIGHT=10 DEFECT, MEASURED. Texture v-period is 10 m; the drawn window row period is 77 px = 3.008 m; marginB = 97 px = 3.789 m of ground-floor shopfront graphics. Exactly 2 window rows fit (sills at 3.789 m and 6.797 m), leaving 1.203 m dead at the top. At every wrap the vertical gap between window rows is 1.203 + 3.789 = 4.99 m against a 3.008 m regular period — and the wrap paints a full ground-floor shopfront (dark glazing bays, fascia sign band, stall riser) plus a parapet cornice (`meshMaterializer.js:294-302`) floating in mid-air. Measured over all 426 baked tiles: 36,122 of 40,828 buildings (88.5%) are >=10 m and show at least one mid-air shopfront band; 12,584 (30.8%) show two. The constant is triple-mirrored at `buildingWorker.js:38-39`, `meshMaterializer.js:25-26`, `buildingRenderer.js:221-222`.

DETAIL STARVATION — THE BIGGEST FINDING. Balconies, cornices and floor bands are gated by `BALCONY_VERT_CAP = 40000` (`buildingWorker.js:43`), checked first-come-first-served in tile order (`:1207`). Simulated against the real footprints in all 158 tiles with >=50 buildings: the MEDIAN tile delivers detail to 26.6% of eligible buildings; p10 is 14.6%; the worst tiles are 8.5-12.4% (33164/24477: 55 of 648; 33160/24473: 81 of 745). 127 of 158 tiles are below 50%. In dense Eixample — exactly where the game looks worst — ~89% of buildings are bare untextured extruded prisms with no roofline cornice at all. Cost driver is the railing: 3 boxes + 11 baluster quads = 58 tris per balcony.

BAKED VERTEX BUDGET IS NOT BINDING (refutes v2-plan-hardened.md:73). Measured wall+roof vertices over every tile: p50 235, p90 18,640, p99 39,380, MAX 46,570. Zero of 426 tiles exceed `GLOBAL_VERTEX_BUDGET = 100000` (`buildingWorker.js:873`). Nothing is dropped today. It becomes binding only after modular bands.

BAKED AO IS APPLIED WRONG. `AO_FACADE_FADE_M = 16` (`buildingWorker.js:219`) is meaningless because the wall quad has vertices ONLY at baseY and topY — the AO interpolates as one linear ramp over the FULL wall height whatever that height is. A 60 m tower is darkened all the way up; median 18 m buildings never reach the intended 16 m cut-off. Constants are mirrored between `aoSampler.js:31,35` and `buildingWorker.js:217-218`. The v9 AO grid is 128x128 over 500 m = 3.9 m cells. `buildingWorker.js:1071` already null-paths the 17 v7 tiles correctly.

DISABLED / DEAD. `ENABLE_DELHI_DETAILS = false` (`buildingWorker.js:92`) kills water tanks, AC units, boundary walls, setback, billboards, garage shutters — intentional, Barcelona migration (changelog.md:39). `renderLODBuildings` is effectively dead: `tileManager.js:3076` shows it only when `!detailLoaded`, and detail is always loaded once a tile finishes phase 2, so it is visible for a handful of frames per tile and then never again. `BUILDING_SIDE = THREE.DoubleSide` (`meshMaterializer.js:562`); a switch to BackSide was attempted 2026-07-06 and reverted (changelog.md:930) because worker winding is inconsistent.

NIGHT. 16 emissive atlases (8 categories x hero) at 1024x1024 RGBA, ALL built at boot by `warmAllBuildingMaterials` (`meshMaterializer.js:971-984`) even in a day session — ~67 MB of canvas backing store, 16 main-thread 2D draw loops, 55.9 MiB measured resident. `renderer.shadowMap.autoUpdate` is never assigned anywhere; the comment at `main.js:969` describes a state the code never establishes, so building walls, roofs and balcony slabs re-render into the shadow map every frame.

BAKED ATTRIBUTES THAT ARE USELESS (checked, so nobody else spends a day on it). `roofShape`, `roofColour`, `material`, `colour` are already in v9 tiles and reach `tileData.buildings`, but coverage is negligible: roofShape 385 of 40,828 (0.94%), material 62 (0.15%), colour 20 (0.05%), minHeight 0. `levels` IS useful: 23,692 (58%), and `buildingNormalize.js:119` derives height = levels x 3.5, so the real storey height is a constant 3.50 m at every percentile (p05 through p95 all 3.50). Meanwhile balconies use floorH = 3.0 (`buildingWorker.js:1210`) and the texture uses 3.008 — three different storey periods on the same wall.

CONFLICT. `map/shopfrontRenderer.js` builds a SEPARATE 3D shopfront (GLASS_TOP 2.55, LINTEL_TOP 2.80, `:18-19`) on the same buildings, so the painted 3.79 m shopfront band and the 3D one overlap and disagree.

## Risks
- ALPHA-TEST EARLY-Z. The railing card is the biggest single triangle win (58 -> 2 tris per balcony) but alpha-tested geometry disables early-Z rejection for the tiles it covers. If railing cards end up net-negative on GPU ms the whole 'detail on 100% of buildings' plan loses its funding. MITIGATE: measure before/after on the worst tile (33160/24473), and keep the modelled railing behind a flag for the nearest LOD tier only.
- SAMPLER2DARRAY ON LAMBERT. three's built-in map uniform is sampler2D; the array path needs an onBeforeCompile chunk swap of map_pars_fragment/map_fragment. We already do heavy shader surgery in getFacadeMaterial (meshMaterializer.js:649-668) so the pattern is proven, but a three minor-version bump could break the string replacements. MITIGATE: pin three, and assert the replacement actually matched (three's replace() silently no-ops on a miss — this is how the aWash attribute failed once before, changelog.md:1016).
- ETC1S ON A HIGH-CONTRAST WINDOW GRID. Basis ETC1S shares chroma endpoints per 4x4 block; hard dark-window-on-light-wall edges ring. At 128 texels/m and car-window viewing distance this is the ETS2 bar and acceptable, but the residential hero layer may need UASTC (8bpp, +1.3 MiB VRAM for that layer). MITIGATE: A/B the two encodes on one layer before authoring all eight.
- BATCHEDMESH DETAIL POOL IS THE HIGH-RISK ITEM (5 days). Per-instance LOD swaps silently no-op unless bm._visibilityChanged is set after setGeometryIdAt (BatchedMesh.js:1185-1194 vs the onBeforeRender early-out at :1507), and the vegPools precedent sets perObjectFrustumCulled=false + sortObjects=false (vegPools.js:45-46) which is exactly the configuration that triggers the bug. MITIGATE: land the vegetation domain's pool fix first and copy it.
- WINDING NORMALISATION HAS ALREADY FAILED ONCE. The 2026-07-06 attempt (changelog.md:930) flipped BUILDING_SIDE to BackSide without fixing the worker and produced hollow buildings; it was reverted to DoubleSide. If it fails again we keep DoubleSide, lose the raster/shadow halving, and the normal-mapped facades still work (normal_fragment_begin flips the derivative TBN under DOUBLE_SIDED) — so this is a perf risk, not a correctness blocker.
- NIGHT IS STRUCTURALLY CAPPED AND MY DOMAIN CANNOT FIX IT. There are zero punctual lights (main.js:192). A normal map's entire visual payoff at night is specular and shadowing response to point lights. My +0.8 ms normal-map spend buys close to nothing after dark unless the lighting domain lands street lighting. If lighting slips, the facade normal map should slip with it and the P2 art should ship albedo-only first.
- GROUND-FLOOR OWNERSHIP IS SPLIT. map/shopfrontRenderer.js builds 3D shopfronts on the same buildings with its own SEG_CAP=260 and MAX_SEGS_PER_BUILDING=4 gates. If the two systems are not reconciled in the same commit we ship two disagreeing shopfronts on the same wall, which is worse than today.
- AUTHORED-FACADE COHERENCE. 8 layers permuted over 40,828 buildings will read as repetition unless the variant selection is block-aware and the dirt-tint attribute does real work. This is an art-direction risk, not a technical one, and it is the difference between 'ETS2' and 'the same building 40,000 times'.
- TIMELINE. 38 days for the domain. If the plan is cut, the highest-value-per-day items in order are: the triangle-budget rewrite (1.5d, takes detail coverage from a median 26.6% to ~100%), the FLOOR_HEIGHT/storey fix inside the modular bands (removes mid-air shopfronts on 88.5% of buildings), roof textures (2d, currently literally zero maps), and shadowMap.autoUpdate (0.1d). The array-texture facade set is the largest single lift and the largest single win, but it is also the only item that hard-depends on an external domain.
